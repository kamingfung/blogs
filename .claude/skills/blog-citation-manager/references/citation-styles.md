# Nature-Adapted Citation Styles Reference Guide

This cheat sheet provides direct copy-paste templates and examples for citing academic and technical sources in your Quarto blog.

---

## 1. Journal Articles

### Template:
`Author, A. B., Author, C. D. & Author, E. F. Article title in sentence case. *Journal Abbr.* **Volume**, StartPage–EndPage (Year). https://doi.org/10.xxxx/yyyy`

### Examples:
- **1 Author**:
  `Lorenz, E. N. Deterministic nonperiodic flow. *J. Atmos. Sci.* **20**, 130–141 (1963). https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1963)020<0130:DNF>2.0.CO;2`
- **2 to 5 Authors**:
  `Fung, K. M., Heald, C. L. & Kroll, J. H. Exploring atmospheric chemical mechanisms for cloud condensation nuclei. *Nature Geosci.* **15**, 123–130 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-022-00914-x`
- **6+ Authors (Rule of 5 $\to$ `et al.`)**:
  `Hersbach, H. et al. The ERA5 global reanalysis. *Q.J.R. Meteorol. Soc.* **146**, 1999–2049 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1002/qj.3803`

---

## 2. Preprints (arXiv, bioRxiv, ChemRxiv, ESSOAr)

### Template:
`Author, A. B. & Author, C. D. Title of preprint. Preprint at *Server Name* https://doi.org/... or https://arxiv.org/abs/... (Year).`

### Examples:
- `Vaswani, A. et al. Attention is all you need. Preprint at *arXiv* https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762 (2017).`
- `Lam, R. et al. Learning skillful medium-range global weather forecasting. Preprint at *arXiv* https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.12794 (2022).`

---

## 3. Books and Handbooks

### Template:
`Author, A. B. *Book Title in Title Case*. (Publisher, City, Year). https://doi.org/...`

### Examples:
- `Stull, R. B. *An Introduction to Boundary Layer Meteorology*. (Springer, Dordrecht, 1988). https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-009-3027-8`
- `Wiggins, G. & McTighe, J. *Understanding by Design*. 2nd edn, (ASCD, Alexandria, VA, 2005).`

---

## 4. Research Datasets

### Template:
`Author, A. B. Dataset Title. *Repository Name* https://doi.org/... (Year).`

### Examples:
- `Hao, Z., AghaKouchak, A., Nakhjiri, N. & Farahmand, A. Global Integrated Drought Monitoring and Prediction System (GIDMaPS) data sets. *figshare* https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.853801 (2014).`
- `Copernicus Climate Change Service. ERA5 hourly data on single levels from 1940 to present. *ECMWF Climate Data Store* https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.adbb2d47 (2023).`

---

## 5. Software Packages & Code Repositories

### Template:
`Author, A. B. / Project Team. Software Title: Version. *Repository/Zenodo* https://doi.org/... or https://github.com/... (Year).`

### Examples:
- `Hoyer, S. & Joseph, H. xarray: N-D labeled arrays and datasets in Python. *J. Open Res. Softw.* **5**, 10 (2017). https://doi.org/10.5334/jors.148`
- `Herzen, J. et al. Darts: User-friendly modern machine learning for time series in Python. *J. Mach. Learn. Res.* **23**, 1–6 (2022). http://jmlr.org/papers/v23/21-1177.html`

---

## 6. Official Reports & Policy Documents

### Template:
`Authoring Body / IPCC. *Report Title: Subtitle*. (Publisher/Organization, City, Year). https://doi.org/...`

### Examples:
- `IPCC. *Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis. Contribution of Working Group I to the Sixth Assessment Report of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change*. (Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, UK, 2021). https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009157896`
