**Promise:** Explain why a cloud droplet falls so slowly that air currents carry it, and why a drop must grow roughly a hundred times wider before it can reach the ground as rain [verify].

**Trap:** Clouds float because they are lighter than air; in fact cloud water is far denser than air, and every droplet is falling, just so slowly that gentle updrafts hold it up and evaporation below the cloud finishes it off.

**Assumes:** cloud-formation/how-a-drop-deforms-in-air (terminal velocity and drag on a falling drop).

## Outline

1. **Every droplet is falling**: gravity pulls, drag pushes back, and a drop quickly reaches a steady fall speed; clouds are made of falling water.
2. **Small drops feel drag far more**: for small drops the fall speed grows with the square of the radius, so ten times smaller means a hundred times slower; typical speeds for a cloud droplet, a drizzle drop and a raindrop [verify].
3. **Updrafts win**: even weak upward air motions exceed a cloud droplet's fall speed, so droplets go where the air goes [verify].
4. **Evaporation below the base**: a droplet that leaves the cloud enters unsaturated air and evaporates within a short distance; only large drops survive the trip, which is why virga exists.
5. **What this means for you**: a cloud overhead is not a rain forecast; rain needs drops large enough to fall and survive, which is why forecasts give a probability of precipitation rather than reading it off cloud cover.
6. **Key takeaways**

## Key takeaways

- Cloud droplets are denser than air and always falling; they stay up because their fall speed is smaller than the vertical air motions around them.
- In the small-drop regime, terminal fall speed grows with the square of the radius, so a typical cloud droplet falls about a centimeter per second [verify].
- A cloud droplet leaving the cloud base evaporates within meters to tens of meters of fall in typical subcloud air [verify].
- A drop needs to reach roughly a millimeter in diameter to fall through an ordinary subcloud layer and reach the ground as rain [verify].
