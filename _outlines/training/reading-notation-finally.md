**Promise:** Read the staff, key signatures and the circle of fifths as a compressed record of the ratios, harmonics and consonances from the earlier modules, and test whether the relearning worked.

**Trap:** Notation is the theory; in fact it is a recording format for physical facts, and read that way it has far less to memorize.

**Assumes:** training/intervals-as-ratios (interval names and the major scale); training/what-the-ear-does-with-a-chord (why some intervals are consonant).

## Outline

1. **The staff as a log-frequency axis**: equal vertical steps are equal ratios, not equal hertz; clefs only choose where the axis starts.
2. **Key signatures as a choice of seven from twelve**: each key is the same pattern of steps moved; a key signature lists the notes that move to keep the pattern.
3. **The circle of fifths is a stack of 3:2**: why adjacent keys differ by one sharp or flat, and why the circle closes only in equal temperament.
4. **Rhythm is ratios too**: note values halve and time signatures group them, the same arithmetic in time instead of pitch.
5. **What this means for you**: the three delayed-retrieval checks from the opening module, run without notes, and how the result will be reported.

## Key takeaways

- On the staff, equal vertical distance means an equal frequency ratio, not an equal frequency difference.
- Each step around the circle of fifths adds or removes exactly one sharp or flat, because moving the scale up a fifth changes one note.
- The circle of fifths closes only because equal temperament absorbs the Pythagorean comma.
- Notation learned after its physical meaning is retained longer than notation learned as symbols. [verify]
